# Structured Phigros authoring v1

Any MCP agent can use this standalone server. It does not parse arbitrary speech or require PhiChain. Default: ONE main playing judgment line at a time. Engine lines may be hidden note carriers (even one note per line), or empty decorative guides. They are not automatically extra playing axes. Carriers resolve onto their declared main anchor at judgment; holds need continuous-path review.

Use chart_capabilities, chart_create/import, authoring_presets/reference, then plan/check/apply small phrases under exact digests. Request chart_read for relevant 8 beats with limit 100 rather than dumping the song. Every plan is prospective; apply is one atomic offline revision and never writes files. Export explicit checkpoints/sidecars for persistence/restart. Stale plan: chart_context, read affected passage, regenerate; never attach a new digest to old assumptions. Export collision: choose a new path; never overwrite.

A cheap repeated workflow: import local precomputed phrase records by path with evidence_import, or cache caller-supplied source/digital analysis with evidence_register(source SHA, settings, producer/version, measured fields); evidence_get returns only relevant fields/pages. Use authoring_plan_phrase with compact motif config/evidence_ref to expand contact scheduling server-side, or choose a preset and exact regular or irregular beats, annotate voice/hand/finger/gesture/rationale, vary lanes/release points deliberately, plan/apply once per phrase. Section is metadata, NOT implicit selection scope. Chart-only tools do not run audio analysis. Optional audio_analyze performs hash/settings-cached register-flux/RMS measurements; it does not hear instruments or decide chart-worthy attacks. evidence_from_chart derives coordinate-free source articulation and explicit carry-in holds with exact BPM/tempo/offset handling; optionally joins audio_ref peaks on an exact beat lattice. Cached inputs retain producer provenance; full official corpus optional.

Patterns are reusable operations, not music generators. Fresh taps represent discrete attacks; holds reserve sustained ownership; drags form travelling contacts; flicks are energetic accents/releases. Actual gesture reuse needs physical target/trajectory review. Raw simultaneous objects, fresh strikes and raw holds are separate counters, NEVER verified independent fingers.

Study: Rrhar'il IN/AT both 1300 notes but different gesture vocabularies. Distorted Fate AT has 1283 versus IN’s 1449; harder does not mean more. Verrückt IN has zero flicks; no four-type quota. Preserve rests, sustained roles and phrase changes. Chronostasis supplies a verified local four-position staircase; it does not prove an entire segment uses persistent 4K lanes.

Coordinates x/y: centered logical canvas fraction; official positionX=18*x, native noteX=1350*x as adapter conventions; actual hitbox/screen scale needs rendered verification. Line position official normalized x/y=.5+logical fraction. Degrees rotation. Public alpha 0..1; native alpha255*value. Public line speed official scroll units; native 4.5*value. Note speed is a multiplier, not beat spacing. Beats exact integers/strings/triples; holds duration in beats. Offset milliseconds. Official hold speed is onset-line-speed adjusted; native holds integrate changing scroll, so moving/changing-speed hold visuals need export comparison.

“Faster” may mean tighter beat spacing, faster approach, or line motion; resolve intent. Flight direction is line orientation/side, not an enforced directional flick field. Boxes, inversion and opposed approaches are special explicit gimmicks, not regular defaults. Read arrangement projected onto a declared main axis with source-local provenance separately from presentation; neither blind local stacking nor generic world-x projection preserves every layout.

Automated checks report sampled logical geometry, event ambiguity, carrier convergence and ownership concerns. They do not simulate complete approach, hold occlusion, human ergonomics or establish official quality. Inspect continuous previews/high-risk sections through an optional renderer, then playtest when available. Future Luna capability is an evaluation goal; this package has no evidence that Luna achieves official-quality charts.

Portable case cards are discoverable with authoring_reference(index): occupied_voice_answer, true_rest_after_tails, reprise_gesture_substitution, carrier_main_locus, terminal_length_duration, recent_lateral_cell and recent_single_axis_decoration. Retrieve one relevant card, then apply its transfer recipe to your own measured music evidence; numeric witnesses and coordinate caveats remain separate from musical/fingering hypotheses.
